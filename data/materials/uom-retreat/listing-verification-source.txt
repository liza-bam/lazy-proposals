Welcome to our Retreat House,

The house is located in central of Dalat, take 7 min to drive to Dalat market, next to Ana Mandara resort. 

You will not only rent a house, the whole garden will be yours, with plenty of plants (avocado, guava, mulberry, tomato..)  and flowers (rose, diamond frost, hydrangea, daisy, cherry blossom...), and vegetables that you could use for your healthy meal.

Wake up at our beautiful place with a hot coffee, feel the birds breeze and let nature touch your soul

The space
PLEASE READ BEFORE BOOKING: 

About our house: 
- 1 private bedroom with 1 queen size bed. 
- The mezzanine with 3 king size mattresses. (We normally arrange only 2 mattresses for group of 6, if you need 3, please give us a note) 
- 2 bathrooms with hot water. 
- Kitchen with basic equipment. 
- Outdoor BBQ + campfire. (we provide firewood)
- Front porch with table to enjoy morning coffee/tea, or meals. 
- Entire garden (you could plant your plant if you want to, please let me know in advance) 

IMPORTANT ALERT:

- You will need to take a little walk to our house, the road might be a little adventure (go up a slope, climb up a stairway - It will be about 100m totally - so plz consider if you have elderly with you) 
- The house is in a big garden, so please beware of the bugs, worms (they're harmless), most commonly mosquitoes. Please wear lengthy clothes and bring your insect repellent
- We have pets live in the property, they're all vaccinated, pest control. Please treat them with kindness. (Please let me know if you want to feed them during your stay, so I could be aware about what they eat).

NEAR BY: 
- 10min to Da Lat market, Da Lat Cathedral, Xuan Huong lake, and other tourist attraction. 
- 5min to Domaine De Marie church, popular Da Lat's street foods. 
- 5min to Van Thanh flower village. 
- 15min to Liang Biang.

Guest access
Entire house + garden

Other things to note
- Towel: we will prepare 1 towel/pax for 1 night booking, 2 towels/pax for 2 nights booking or more. 

- Bathroom utilities: we will prepare toilet paper, shampoo, shower gel, toothpaste. We dont have single use toothbrush and comb, so please prepare them yourself. 

- EARLY CHECK-IN and LATE CHECK-OUT will depend on the previous/next guest. We could only confirm in the morning of your check-in/out date. 

- There will be a fee for late check-out (300.000VND/h).
